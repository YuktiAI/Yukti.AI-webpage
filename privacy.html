Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 31, 2026 | Effective Date: July 31, 2026

1. Introduction

YUKTII AI LABS ("Company", "we", "us", or "our"), headquartered in Bengaluru, Karnataka, India, respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy informs you how we collect, process, store, and safeguard data when you visit our website (www.yuktiiai.in) or engage our AI engineering and software development services.

2. Data We Collect

We collect information directly provided by you through form submissions, email inquiries, or project discovery discussions:

  • Identity Data: Name, job title, company name.
  • Contact Data: Email address, phone number.
  • Technical Project Data: System requirements, tech stack details, and business challenges submitted via our contact forms.
  • Usage Data: IP address, browser type, device information via essential analytics.

3. How We Use Your Data

Your information is processed strictly for legitimate business purposes:

  • To evaluate engineering challenges and respond to project inquiries.
  • To prepare proposals, system architecture reviews, and statements of work.
  • To fulfill contract requirements and deliver software development services.
  • To comply with applicable laws, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDP Act, 2023).

4. Client Confidentiality & AI Model Isolation

We enforce strict data isolation protocols. Proprietary business data, source code, and confidential documents shared with YUKTII AI LABS under NDA are never used to train public or shared foundation models. Client datasets remain strictly isolated within designated enterprise cloud environments.

5. Data Security & Retention

We implement enterprise-grade security controls, including TLS 1.3 encryption in transit, AES-256 encryption at rest, and strict role-based access controls. We retain personal data only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes for which it was collected or to satisfy legal and accounting requirements.

6. Your Rights

Under the DPDP Act 2023 and applicable privacy regulations, you have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data held by YUKTII AI LABS. To exercise these rights, contact us at connect@yuktiiai.in.
