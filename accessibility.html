Inclusive Engineering

Accessibility Statement

Target Standard: WCAG 2.1 Level AA

1. Our Commitment

YUKTII AI LABS is committed to ensuring digital accessibility for people of all abilities. We continuously improve the user experience for everyone and apply relevant accessibility standards across our website and software products.

2. Measures Taken

  • Keyboard Navigation: All interactive elements feature visible focus indicators (`focus-visible`) and logical tab sequence.
  • Skip Link: Direct keyboard skip link to bypass main header navigation.
  • Reduced Motion: Complete animation disablement when `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` is set.
  • Semantic HTML: Explicit landmark roles, heading hierarchy, and ARIA labels.
  • Contrast Ratios: Text elements tuned for high-contrast readability against dark surface containers.

3. Feedback

We welcome your feedback on the accessibility of the YUKTII AI LABS website. If you encounter accessibility barriers, please notify us at connect@yuktiiai.in.
